# Neural Networks: Zero to Hero

My personal implementations, experiments, and notes from Andrej Karpathy's [Neural Networks: Zero to Hero](https://github.com/karpathy/nn-zero-to-hero) course.

The goal of this repository is to develop a deeper understanding of neural networks by implementing their core components from scratch.

## Projects

### 1. Micrograd

A minimal autograd engine implementing automatic differentiation and backpropagation.

**Key concepts:**
- Computational graphs
- Chain rule and backpropagation
- Gradient computation
- Building neural networks from scratch

**Implementation:** `engine.py`, `nn.py`, `test_engine.py`

### 2. Makemore

Building character-level language models step by step.

**Topics explored:**
- Bigram language models
- Neural network language modeling
- Word embeddings
- Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLPs)
- Weight initialization
- Batch normalization
- Training and evaluation

**Implementation:** `makemore/`

## Learning Goals

- Understand how neural networks work under the hood.
- Implement fundamental deep learning algorithms from scratch.
- Gain practical experience with PyTorch.
- Develop a foundation for further study of modern language models and Transformers.
